I have rebuilt this website from scratch. The old one was a single page. This one is a place to publish.

## What you will find here

I work in two worlds. By day I run high-performance computing infrastructure for a university. By training I am a researcher, with a PhD in Computing Science from the University of Glasgow. This site brings both together:

- **How-to guides** on Linux, HPC, storage, networking and automation, written from systems I actually run.
- **Field reports** from conferences, workshops and collaboration meetings.
- **First looks** at technology I am testing.
- **Research, explained plainly**, starting with my own work on network function virtualisation at the edge.
- **Career advice** for people who want to work in infrastructure engineering or move between research and industry.

## How to follow

New posts appear on the [writing page](/writing/) and in the [RSS feed](/feed.xml). Videos from my [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@harunaadoga) show up on the [media page](/media/) on their own.

If something here is useful, or wrong, [tell me](/contact/). I would like to hear from you either way.
